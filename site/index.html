    If a relative calls or writes asking for money

    • Voices can be copied from a few seconds of audio. Hang up and call them back on the number you already have.
    • Agree a safe phrase with your family now, at least four words, and never post it.
    • Ask for the phrase before sending anything. If they cannot give it, do not pay.
